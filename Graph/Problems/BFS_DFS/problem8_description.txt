Plumbers are trying to map the intricate, entirely undocumented pipe network of a historical 19th-century mansion to find the source of a massive concealed leak. 

They pour a harmless, glowing ultraviolet dye into the main water intake valve in the basement. The technicians then need to methodically check every single faucet, drain, and exposed connecting pipe in the building to see exactly where the dye has spread. 

They must systematically mark off each checked room and fixture on their physical blueprint. Tracking their progress ensures they systematically trace the flow through the entire connected plumbing system, verifying all reachable outlets without repeatedly checking the same bathrooms.
