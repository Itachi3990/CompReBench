A commercial airline serves over a hundred airports worldwide. A direct flight between two airports has a specific ticket price, but sometimes it is much cheaper for a passenger to book a multi-leg journey with layovers. 

The airline is building a new consumer-facing booking engine. To provide the fastest user experience, they want to pre-calculate the absolute cheapest itinerary between every possible combination of departure and destination airports they service. 

This master matrix of minimum ticket prices will allow the booking engine to instantly display the best deals to users. The algorithm must evaluate all possible multi-stop routes to find the lowest possible cost for travel between all pairs of airports in their flight network.
