A major Internet Service Provider (ISP) is routing live streaming traffic for a global sporting event. The data originates at a central server farm and needs to reach a massive regional distribution gateway. 

The fiber-optic cables connecting the various routers and switches in the backbone network each have specific bandwidth limits (e.g., 10 Gbps, 40 Gbps). To prevent buffering and network crashes, the network architects must calculate the theoretical maximum amount of data per second that can be routed from the server farm to the gateway across all available parallel and intersecting pathways.
