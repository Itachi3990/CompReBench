In the late 33rd century, the Galactic Terran Alliance (GTA) has successfully colonized over four hundred star systems scattered across the Orion Spur. For decades, travel between these systems relied on conventional warp drives, a slow and perilous method that left outer colonies isolated and vulnerable to resource shortages. To usher in a new era of prosperity and centralized governance, the Galactic Senate has approved the "Aegis Unity Project," a monumental engineering endeavor to construct a permanent, instantaneous jump-gate network linking every colonized star system. 

The technology behind these jump-gates relies on generating and stabilizing macroscopic Einstein-Rosen bridges—wormholes—using an incredibly rare and volatile substance known as 'Exotic Matter-9'. The Astrogation Guild, a faction of elite navigators and physicists, has spent the last five years painstakingly mapping the subspace topology of the Orion Spur. Their automated surveyor drones have identified all theoretically viable jump-gate links between pairs of star systems, navigating treacherous cosmic anomalies to gather data. 

However, not all links are created equal. The amount of Exotic Matter-9 required to establish and permanently stabilize a wormhole between two specific systems varies drastically. It depends heavily on the real-space distance, the density of dark matter in the intervening space, gravitational interference from nearby celestial bodies like black holes or pulsars, and complex, unpredictable subspace weather patterns. The Astrogation Guild has compiled a massive, comprehensive ledger detailing the exact Exotic Matter-9 expenditure required for every single possible jump-gate link in the sector.

The Galactic Senate faces a critical dilemma that threatens the entire project. Their strategic reserves of Exotic Matter-9 are strictly limited, extracted at immense financial and human cost from the volatile coronas of dying stars. Yet, the political mandate of the Aegis Unity Project is absolute and non-negotiable: every single one of the four hundred colonized star systems must be integrated into the jump-gate network. A citizen, merchant vessel, or military fleet must be able to travel from any system in the Alliance to any other system, even if it requires traversing multiple intermediate jump-gates along the way. Leaving even a minor, resource-poor colony isolated would trigger a severe political crisis, sparking rebellion and threatening the fragile stability of the Alliance.

The Chief Engineer of the project, stationed on the capital world of Earth, has been tasked with an immense computational and logistical challenge. They must analyze the Astrogation Guild's ledger and select a specific subset of the proposed jump-gate links to actually construct. This network must encompass all four hundred star systems, ensuring complete galactic connectivity. Crucially, the total Exotic Matter-9 cost of the selected links must be the absolute minimum possible. There is no room for error or excess.

Furthermore, the Senate Oversight Committee has imposed a strict "No Redundancy" protocol, enforced by threat of treason. Exotic Matter-9 is far too precious to waste on alternative or backup routes. The final network design must not contain any closed loops or cycles. If there is already a sequence of jump-gates allowing travel between System A and System B, constructing an additional, direct link between them is considered an egregious waste of galactic resources and is strictly forbidden. The network must be perfectly continuous but minimally connected. 

The resulting structure will be fragile; if any single jump-gate in the final design were to fail, it would instantly fracture the network into two isolated segments, cutting off billions of citizens. Despite this risk, the economic constraints demand this exact architectural approach. The Chief Engineer's ultimate goal is to find the single most efficient, cost-minimizing configuration of jump-gates that binds the entire galactic civilization together without a single ounce of wasted Exotic Matter-9, balancing the survival of the colonies with the severe limitations of their technology.
