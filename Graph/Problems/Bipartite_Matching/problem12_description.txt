Warehouse Picker Plan

A warehouse is assigning pickers to picking zones. The left side of the graph lists pickers, and the right side lists zones. A connection means a picker is qualified to work that zone. Each picker can be assigned to only one zone, and each zone can be assigned to only one picker.

The warehouse supervisor wants the maximum number of valid assignments under those rules. A picker with only one feasible zone should be protected from unnecessary conflicts, while a flexible picker may need to be matched strategically to avoid reducing the total count. The best solution is the one that produces the largest possible set of non-overlapping picker-zone pairings.

The objective is maximum one-to-one pairing across two groups connected by compatibility edges.
