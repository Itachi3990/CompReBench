A game studio is developing a complex dungeon crawler with hundreds of rooms connected by one-way drop-offs, teleporters, and magical sliding doors that lock behind the player. 

Before finalizing the level design and shipping the game, the quality assurance team needs to ensure players don't get permanently trapped in a dead-end section of the dungeon. 

They want an automated map analysis tool to segment the dungeon layout into distinct "safe zones." The tool must process the directional connections and identify maximal clusters of rooms where a player can freely travel from any room to any other room within that specific cluster, ensuring they can completely explore that zone without fear of a soft-lock.
