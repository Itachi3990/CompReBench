A search engine company is analyzing the hyperlink structure of a vast segment of the internet. Pages link to other pages, but these links are strictly one-way. 

To better understand echo chambers and closely-knit online communities, the researchers want to segment the internet into distinct functional groups. They need an automated tool to process billions of pages and identify isolated groups of websites. 

Specifically, they are looking for maximal clusters where every single page in the group can eventually reach every other page in that same group by following a sequence of hyperlinks. Finding these mutually reachable clusters will reveal deeply interconnected digital subcultures that share traffic exclusively among themselves.
