A global manufacturing conglomerate tracks the flow of specialized components between its hundreds of factories. Factory A might ship parts to Factory B, which then uses those parts to build a module shipped to Factory C. 

The logistics team wants to identify potential vulnerabilities in this directed flow of materials. They are particularly worried about systemic bottlenecks that could halt global production. 

They need an algorithm to find specific clusters of factories that are completely mutually dependent. This means they are searching for maximal sets of factories where, through a convoluted chain of shipments, every factory in the cluster ultimately relies on parts originating from every other factory in that cluster, creating a dangerous closed loop of dependency.
