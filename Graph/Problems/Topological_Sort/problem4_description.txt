A head chef is preparing a wildly complex, multi-course banquet. The recipe involves dozens of individual tasks: marinating meat, chopping vegetables, creating sauces, baking bread, and plating. Many tasks have strict dependencies: the dough must rise before it is baked, and the sauce must be reduced before it is poured over the meat. 

To manage the kitchen staff efficiently, the chef needs to lay out a master timeline. They must order all the tasks chronologically so that no cook is ever waiting on a step that hasn't been started, ensuring a smooth, linear workflow.
