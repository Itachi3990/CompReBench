An automotive manufacturing plant is designing a new assembly line for an electric vehicle. The car consists of thousands of parts that must be assembled in a specific order. The battery pack must be installed before the seats, and the chassis must be welded before the battery is inserted. 

The factory engineers need to take this complex web of assembly dependencies and flatten it into a single, step-by-step sequential instruction manual for the robotic assembly arms. This strict chronological list guarantees that no manufacturing step is attempted before its physical prerequisites are met.
